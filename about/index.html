Our story

We know the gate the stock arrives at.

We build software for South African workshops, factories and trade suppliers. Places where stock arrives at the gate every day, with a delivery note in someone’s hand.

We know that gate. Francois spent more than a decade running a family business before he became a full-time engineer. Donovan built his first stock-control app for a workshop years ago, long before the tools we use today existed. Between us we have sat in the stores, argued with suppliers about short deliveries, and typed the same delivery note into two systems.

Then we built a full stock, purchasing and job-card system for a South African cold-room manufacturer. It runs their business every day. While we were there we saw the same thing we had seen everywhere else: the paperwork arrives faster than anyone can capture it, stock levels lag by days, and the person doing the receiving loses most of their day to it.

So we built the thing we wished we had. Photograph the supplier paperwork. The system reads the line items, matches them to your stock codes, checks them against the order, and books the stock. Every correction you make teaches it your suppliers. It gets better the longer you use it.

We are based in Cape Town. We are engineers first. We answer the phone.

What we have built

Systems that are running right now.

  • A stock and operations system in daily use at a South African manufacturer.

    Stock, suppliers, purchase orders, quotes, job cards, delivery notes and customers, connected to their accounting package. It is the same system we now offer as our ERP module.

  • Document-reading software inside a large, regulated finance company.

    We designed and shipped a system that reads thousands of documents a day, checks every field against the company’s records, and leaves a full audit trail. That is the same approach behind our Document Intake module.

  • Three years as the technology team for a fast-growing South African trading group.

    We built their platform, ran their infrastructure, and kept it up through their busiest periods.

  • Our own products.

    A caregiver marketplace in the UK, an app for a South African recycling business, and a visual search app for fashion, all live and used by paying customers.

We have built for businesses from five people to several hundred. We integrate with what you already run. We do not make you throw anything away.
