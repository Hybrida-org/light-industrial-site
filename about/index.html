Who we are

We are a four-person team in Cape Town.

We build operations software for South African workshops, factories and trade suppliers — stock, purchasing, job cards and supplier paperwork.

Between us we have been building software for over ten years. We have built both of our modules into one working system for a cold-room manufacturer, who runs their business on it.

You will deal with the people who built it. There is no support tier, and nobody hands you over after the sale.

What is running

  • A full stock and operations system, with document intake, in daily use at a South African cold-room manufacturer.
  • Document-reading software inside a large, regulated finance company.
  • Three years as the technology team for a growing South African trading group.
  • Our own products: a caregiver marketplace in the UK, a recycling app, and a visual search app for fashion.

We have built for businesses from five people to several hundred, and we work with what you already run.
