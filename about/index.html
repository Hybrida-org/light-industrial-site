What we have built

  • A manufacturing ERP with QuickBooks integration, live at a South African manufacturer. Core ERP grew out of it.
  • AI document validation inside a listed UK consumer-finance lender, reading tens of thousands of fields in production.
  • Three years as the technology team for a South African precious-metals group while it grew three times over.
  • The project-management system for a construction contractor building housing, bridges and hospitals.
