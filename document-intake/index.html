What it reads

We read supplier quotes, invoices and delivery notes line by line

Send the document the way it arrives. We read every line, match it to your codes, check it against the paperwork before it, and book the result.

Supplier quotes

  • Every line, quantity, unit and price, plus the reference and validity date.
  • Quotes from different suppliers line up side by side in your codes.
  • Accept one and it becomes the purchase order everything else is checked against.

Supplier invoices

  • Number, date, VAT and every line, multi-page included.
  • Checked against the order and the delivery before you pay. A moved price or an extra line is flagged.
  • Cost prices update on your stock items.

Delivery notes

  • Matched line by line to the order. Booked in full or short, with the balance on record.
  • Pack sizes convert to your units. A box of 100 books as 100.
  • The photo is filed against the stock movement as proof of delivery.

How documents reach us

  • A phone photo from the yard or the counter.
  • An email forwarded to your own intake address, attachments and all.
  • A PDF or scan dropped into the app, multi-page included.

File types and conditions

  • PDF, JPG, PNG and HEIC. Scans and photos.
  • Skew, shadow and creases are fine. Handwritten corrections are read.
  • Anything uncertain is flagged for a one-tap confirm. It never guesses.

What it learns per supplier

  • Each supplier's layout, item names and pack sizes, after the first confirm.
  • The same line never needs matching twice. New ones are approved once.
  • Processed in South Africa and kept with the movement they created. See data handling.
