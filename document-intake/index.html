How it works

From the delivery note to stock on hand, in three steps.

It runs on the documents your suppliers already send you, so there is nothing to set up on their side. No portal to sign them up for, and no asking them to send their paperwork a particular way.

  1. 01

    Photograph it

    A delivery note, a supplier invoice or a quote, photographed on a phone. Every line on it gets read: code, description, quantity, unit and price.

  2. 02

    We match it to your stock

    Every supplier names and counts things their own way. Tell the system once how theirs lines up with yours and it holds from then on — so gloves that come in boxes of a hundred book as two hundred pairs, because that is how you count them.

  3. 03

    We check it, then book it

    Anything that does not match what you ordered — a price that moved, a quantity that came up short — is flagged for you before it is paid. You decide what happens: book it in full, book it short, or turn it away. Every decision you make is kept, so the next delivery from that supplier asks less of you than the last one. Stock on hand and your costs update, with the photo filed against the movement.

For the first month or two you will be confirming matches as new supplier lines come through. After that most documents book themselves.

What it reads

The paperwork you already get, in whatever shape it arrives.

Supplier quotes

  • PDF, photo, scan, or the text sitting in the body of an email.
  • Lines, quantities and prices come out as a list you can compare against the other quotes you asked for.
  • Every price is kept, so you can see what this supplier charged you last time.

Delivery notes

  • Photographed on a phone when the delivery arrives.
  • Matched line by line to the order it belongs to.
  • Booked in full, short, or turned away on the spot, with the photo kept on the movement as your proof.

Invoices

  • Checked against both what you ordered and what actually came off the truck.
  • A price that moved, a quantity that does not match, or a line nobody ordered is flagged before the invoice is paid.

An audit trail nobody has to keep

  • Every document is filed against the supplier, the order and the stock movement it created.
  • When a count is queried months later, the photo of the note that moved it is one click away.
