What it reads

Supplier quotes, invoices and delivery notes, read line by line

Send the document the way it arrives. We pull out every line, match it to your own stock codes, suppliers and units, check it against the paperwork that came before it, and book the result. Three document types, three jobs.

Supplier quotes

  • Every line, quantity, unit and price comes out as a list, with the supplier's reference and validity date.
  • Quotes from different suppliers for the same job line up side by side in your codes, so you compare like with like.
  • Accept one and it becomes the purchase order the invoice and delivery note are checked against.

Supplier invoices

  • Invoice number, date, VAT and every line read, including multi-page invoices and credit lines.
  • Checked against what you ordered and what arrived. A price that moved, a quantity that differs or a line nobody ordered is flagged before you pay.
  • Cost prices update on your stock items, so your margins use what you actually paid.

Delivery notes

  • Photographed at the gate and matched line by line to the order. Booked in full, or short, with what is still outstanding on record.
  • Supplier pack sizes convert to your units. A box of 100 books as 100, a 6 m length books as 6 m.
  • The photo is filed against the stock movement, so proof of delivery is never a piece of paper in a bakkie.

How documents reach us

  • Photograph it on a phone, straight from the yard or the counter.
  • Forward the supplier's email to your own intake address. Attachments are read, the email body too.
  • Drag a PDF or a scan into the app. Multi-page files are handled as one document.

File types and conditions

  • PDF, JPG, PNG and HEIC, scans and photos.
  • Skew, shadow, creases and a thumb in the corner are fine. Handwritten quantities and corrections on a printed document are read.
  • Anything the system is not certain of is flagged for a one-tap confirm. It never guesses a quantity or a price.

What it learns per supplier

  • Each supplier's layout, item names and pack sizes are remembered after the first confirm.
  • The same supplier line never needs matching twice. New lines are proposed, approved once, then remembered.
  • Documents are processed in South Africa and kept with the stock movement they created. See the data handling page.
