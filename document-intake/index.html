How it works

How Document Intake reads a supplier delivery note

AI reads the document, matches each line to your stock codes and flags anything that does not add up. It runs on the documents your suppliers already send you. There is nothing for them to set up.

  1. Photograph the delivery note or forward the email

    Photograph the delivery note on a phone, or forward the email. A PDF, a scan or a photo all go in the same way.

  2. We read every line and match it to your stock codes

    Every line comes off the document and is matched to your stock codes, your suppliers and your units. A box of a hundred gloves books as two hundred pairs, because that is how you count them.

  3. Your stock updates and odd lines are flagged

    Stock on hand and costs update, with the document filed against the movement. A price that moved, a short quantity or a line nobody ordered is flagged for a one-tap check.

For the first month or two you will be confirming matches as new supplier lines come through. After that most documents book themselves.
