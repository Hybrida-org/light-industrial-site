This policy explains what personal information the Light Industrial website collects, why, who handles it, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

It covers the website at lightindustrial.co.za only. When you become a customer, the subscription agreement and the data handling page explain how we treat the documents and stock data you put through the software.

What we collect and where

We collect personal information in five places on this site.

The contact form

The form on the Contact page, the bottom of the Home page and the Document Intake page asks for your name, company, work email, phone number, how many delivery notes you receive a week, what runs your stock today, and a consent tick. The consent tick is a record that you agreed to be contacted about your enquiry.

The hero form

The short form at the top of the Home page asks for your work email and phone number only.

The savings calculator

The calculator on the Savings page works out what supplier paperwork costs you. To see the full result you enter your work email. We keep the email together with the slider values you set (documents per week, hourly rate and similar), because they tell us what to prepare for a conversation with you.

Calendly bookings

"Book a 30-min demo" opens Calendly in a new tab. Calendly asks for your name, email and the time you choose. Calendly collects this under its own privacy policy and then sends the booking to us.

Cookies and similar technologies

With your consent, analytics and marketing tags record how you use the site: pages viewed, buttons clicked, approximate location, device and browser. The Cookies section lists each one. Our hosting provider also keeps short-lived server logs that include your IP address.

If you email us, we keep the email and your address in our mailbox for as long as the enquiry needs.

Why we use it and the lawful basis

POPIA lets us process personal information only where a justification in section 11 applies. Ours are:

  • To answer your enquiry and run a demo. We use your form details to reply, to book and prepare a demo, and to send a quote. Justification: steps you asked us to take before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us.
  • To send marketing about Light Industrial. We only email you about the product if you ticked the consent box or you are a customer, as section 69 of POPIA requires. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To measure and improve the site. Analytics tags run only after you accept analytics cookies. Justification: consent.
  • To measure advertising. Google Ads, Meta and LinkedIn tags run only after you accept marketing cookies. Justification: consent.
  • To keep the site secure. Hosting logs help us spot abuse. Justification: legitimate interest.

We do not sell personal information. We do not make automated decisions about you that have a legal effect.

Cross-border transfers

Most of the processors above store information outside South Africa. Section 72 of POPIA allows this where the recipient is subject to a law, binding corporate rules or a contract that protects the information in a way substantially similar to POPIA, or where you have consented, or where the transfer is needed for a contract with you.

HubSpot stores our records in the European Union under the GDPR. The United States providers above are bound by their standard data processing terms, which include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Where a tag runs only on your consent, that consent also covers the transfer it causes.

Consent and how to change it

On your first visit a banner offers three choices: accept all, only necessary, or cookie settings. Nothing beyond necessary runs until you choose. Your choice is kept in li_consent and sent to Google Consent Mode, which keeps the Google tags in line with it.

To change your mind, click Cookie settings in the footer of any page. Withdrawing consent stops new tracking from that point. It does not delete what a vendor already holds, but you can ask us to arrange that under Your rights.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and leads. Kept while the enquiry is open and for 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted from HubSpot.
  • Customers. Kept for the life of the subscription agreement and as long as tax and company law require afterwards.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics keeps user-level data for 14 months. Other vendors keep data for their documented defaults.
  • Hosting logs. Cloudflare keeps request logs for a short period measured in days.
  • Email. Kept while the conversation is live, then deleted on the same 24-month cycle as leads.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel over HTTPS to a Cloudflare Worker and then to HubSpot, and are not stored on the website itself. Access to HubSpot is limited to the people who handle sales and support, each with their own login and two-factor authentication. We review who has access when someone joins or leaves.

If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as section 22 of POPIA requires.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and ask for a copy (access),
  • ask us to correct or update it (correction),
  • ask us to delete it where we no longer have a reason to keep it (deletion),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, and object to direct marketing at any time (objection),
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before,
  • complain to the Information Regulator.

To exercise a right, email donovan@lightindustrial.co.za with "POPIA request" in the subject. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We respond within 30 days. Access requests may be made on the Regulator's Form 2 under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, but a plain email is enough for us.
