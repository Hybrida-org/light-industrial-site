What Core ERP stock control software does.

Stock records and supplier prices

  • One record per item with your own codes. A price per supplier per item, with the history of what you paid.

Purchase orders and receiving stock

  • Raise the order, PDF it, email it. Receive against it, partial deliveries included, and the stock updates.

Quotes with product bundles and your units

  • Group parts into a product so the customer sees one line and you see the parts list. Buy in sheets, quote in metres.

Job cards from approved quotes

  • Open a job from the quote. Issue parts to it. See what it cost against what you quoted.

Delivery notes and returns

  • The delivery note comes off the quote, so nothing is retyped. Returns go back into stock with a reason.

Stock take and audit trail

  • Count, adjust, done. Every movement is logged, so you can always see why a number changed.

Accounting integration

  • Integrates with QuickBooks, Xero and Sage. Your accountant keeps their system.
