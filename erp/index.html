What it does

What Core ERP stock control software does.

We digitise the whole warehouse: every item, every count, every movement, from the phone in the storeman's hand to the books. Stock, buying, quoting, jobs and delivery in one system, with a full audit trail.

Stock records and supplier prices

  • One record per item with your own codes, units and bin location. Raw materials, consumables, finished goods and assemblies. A price per supplier per item, with the history of what you paid.
  • The last delivered price sits on the order line, so the next order starts from what you actually paid.

Purchase orders and receiving stock

  • Raise the order, PDF it, email it. Receive against it, partial deliveries included, and the stock updates.
  • Add Document Intake and the supplier's delivery note books the receipt for you.

Quotes with bills of materials and your units

  • Build a product from its bill of materials (BOM). The customer sees one line, you see the parts list and the cost.
  • Quote in the units you sell and buy in the units you stock. Buy in sheets, quote in metres.
  • Working from a bill of quantities (BOQ)? Load it and quote from it, and we raise the purchase orders per supplier from the same list.
  • Accept the quote and it becomes the job, with nothing retyped.

Job cards and work orders from approved quotes

  • Open a job from the quote. Issue parts to it. See what it cost against what you quoted.
  • Build your own products too: a build job card consumes the BOM parts and puts the finished good on the shelf at its parts cost.
  • Finished goods, assemblies and sub-assemblies sit in stock like any other item, so you can sell from the shelf or build to order.

Delivery notes and returns

  • The delivery note comes off the quote, so nothing is retyped. Delivered deducts the stock.
  • Returns go back into stock with a reason, and the credit is on record.

Stock take on a phone, every movement on record

  • Count the warehouse on a phone, bin by bin. Draft counts save as you go, so a count can run over a few days without losing anything.
  • Before a count is posted, anything that moved since it was counted is flagged. Then the storeman posts it and stock is corrected in one go.
  • Every movement is logged with who, when and why, so a number on the screen can always be traced back to a document or a count.

Accounting integration

  • Integrates with QuickBooks, Xero and Sage. Your accountant keeps their system.
  • Customers, suppliers and invoices stay in step, without double capture.

AI built in, working on your data

  • Ask it what is low, what a job cost, or who you paid most last quarter. It drafts purchase orders from what is running short and you approve them.
