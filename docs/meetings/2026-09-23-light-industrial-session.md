## Decisions

1. **ERP and Document Intake each get their own full page.** Ads can point straight at them, each page carries the lead form, and the extra content helps search engines find us.
2. **The home page must explain the product in about 20 seconds.** Everything deeper moves behind a "read more" link to its own page (progressive disclosure).
3. **The two modules are priced separately.** Document Intake starts at R2,500 a month, which covers 50 documents. Above that the price climbs steeply. ERP is added as a separate line item.
4. **"Up to five users" is the real limit** — the current "every person on your team, never charged per user" line is wrong and must go.
5. **The setup fee comes off the pricing section.**
6. **Finish one task before starting the next.** Too much is sitting half-done at once.
7. **Fireflies transcripts get sent to Donovan** so he can file them in the team Obsidian knowledge base.
