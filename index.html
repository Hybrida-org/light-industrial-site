The daily grind

Where the day gets lost.

Most light-industrial businesses run on email, spreadsheets and a disconnected accounting package — and paperwork is the bottleneck. Three places it costs you every week.

Manual re-entry

Typed by hand, twice.

Someone keys every quote and invoice into stock and accounts by hand — slow, and wrong often enough to matter.

Mismatched counts

The shelf and the system disagree.

The system says one thing, the shelf says another. Nobody fully trusts the on-hand number when it matters.

Month-end

Days lost matching paper.

Delivery notes to orders to invoices, by hand, to catch what was missed — every single month.

Smart capture

It reads the paperwork and understands it — not just scans it.

Behind every photo, four things happen to turn a picture into clean, matched data.

Suppliers

Matched to your suppliers

We match it to suppliers you already have, so you don't get duplicates. Anything new is proposed for you to approve — never added on its own.

Matched records No duplicates Propose new

Line items

Matched to your stock

Each line is matched to your stock list. Anything it can't place comes up with a suggested match to accept or fix.

Smart match Suggested match One-tap confirm

Units

Boxes become units

Your supplier sells in boxes; you count in units. We convert it as goods arrive, so stock reads the way you actually use it.

Boxes to units Converted on arrival Accurate counts

Receipts

Receive part of an order

Got some of the order? Receive what arrived and keep the rest open. Stock shows what's really on the shelf, not what was ordered.

Full or partial Stays open Audit trail

How it works

Snap it. We read it. Your stock updates.

The whole loop runs on the documents your suppliers already send — no new system for them, no retyping for you.

01

Raise a purchase order

Create a PO and send it to your supplier.

02

Supplier replies

A quote comes back as a PDF, an email, or a photo. OCR reads the document.

03

Normalise & match

Captured lines are matched to your stock items and suppliers. Anything unknown is flagged for a one-click confirm.

04

Accept the quote

Approve it and the quote becomes an approved order, ready to receive against.

05

Capture the receipt

Snap the delivery note when goods arrive. OCR reconciles it against the order — full or partial.

06

Stock updates itself

Levels rise automatically on receipt, in the units you actually count, with a full audit trail.
